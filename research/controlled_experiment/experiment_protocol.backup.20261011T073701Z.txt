AegisOps controlled research experiment
=======================================

Target: 40 fault episodes
Fault classes: 20 latency; 20 HTTP-error
Controls: Healthy-only trials recorded separately

Primary evaluation:
1. Detection performance by detector and fault class.
2. Detection latency from injection to first alert.
3. False alarms during verified healthy periods.
4. Root-cause diagnosis accuracy against the known injected fault.
5. Recovery success based on independent post-action verification.
6. Time to verified recovery, plus failed or incomplete trials.

Experimental safeguards:
- Do not use individual telemetry samples as independent fault episodes.
- Do not count an approval or action-completed message as proof of recovery.
- Record raw model, rule-based, and combined decisions separately.
- Record every eligible trial, including missed detections and failed recovery.
- Record diagnosis before revealing the known fault label to the evaluator where practical.
- Use explicitly approved local-demo recovery only.
- Do not overwrite the existing database or model checkpoint.
- Preserve timestamps in UTC and document any missing measurements.

Important:
This file is an initial protocol outline, not a completed experiment.
Freeze thresholds and comparison procedures before the evaluation run.
