# AegisOps Phase 5 Readiness Verdict

**Verdict: B — literature/reference inventory completed, but material citation, claim-scope, and evidence-boundary issues remain.**

## What is ready

- The manuscript’s active 16-entry bibliography has been inventoried and individually checked against primary or authoritative records to the extent accessible.
- The literature landscape establishes the relevant methods as prior art and supports a conservative framing of AegisOps as an engineering integration with bounded experiments.
- Claim-to-evidence mapping preserves the canonical A/B/C/live/D boundaries and does not merge snapshots, datasets, or experiments.

## Highest-priority blockers before publication review

1. **No external result:** AnoMod archive provenance is not performance validation. Production predictions, metrics, ratification, and contract-valid execution remain absent/blocked.
2. **No live LSTM result:** all 200 live records used bootstrap rules and have null LSTM scores. Any live LSTM accuracy, discrimination, false-alarm, or generalization wording is unsupported.
3. **Approval claims must remain local:** Experiment C only supports the defined local harness. The implementation has no authenticated two-person authorization and cannot be described as NIST AC-3(2) compliant, production secure, or autonomously safe.
4. **Literature does not establish the pilot’s effectiveness:** AutoGen and MetaGPT establish generic frameworks, not AIOps diagnosis efficacy; six scenarios and two `UNASSESSABLE` omissions do not establish causal LLM benefit.
5. **Reference cleanup required:** correct/check the AnoMod plural title and OmniAnomaly singular title; add sources or narrow uncited Shewhart/Holt-Winters, SRE-verification, and guarded-remediation assertions.

## Release condition

This verdict does not authorize a manuscript edit. A future correction pass may proceed only after preserving the source map in the Phase 5 CSVs and applying the wording boundaries in `PHASE5_GAPS_AND_RECOMMENDED_WORDING.md`.
