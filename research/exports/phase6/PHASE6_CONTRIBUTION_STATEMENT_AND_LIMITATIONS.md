# AegisOps Phase 6 — Conservative Contribution Statement and Limitations

## Recommended contribution statement for a later correction pass

AegisOps is best presented as a **source-audited engineering integration** of (i) a frozen LSTM-autoencoder and deterministic-rule anomaly-detection design, (ii) configurable diagnostic workflow components, and (iii) a local approval-gated recovery demonstration. Its present evidence supports a structured synthetic detection benchmark, a small configuration-specific diagnosis pilot, and a bounded local safety-harness evaluation. It does not establish live-LSTM performance, externally validated detection, causal diagnosis, authenticated dual control, production security, or autonomous safe recovery.

## Claim classification

| Category | What may be stated | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Established prior art | LSTM autoencoders, isolation forests, rules, graph/probabilistic RCA, and generic LLM-agent frameworks are established methods. | Cite verified literature; do not call the LSTM architecture novel. |
| Engineering integration | The repository combines these components with defined scopes and audit artifacts. | Source traceability and implementation evidence; distinguish configured from observed behavior. |
| Supported empirical contributions | A: 320 synthetic windows; B: 42 records with ITT omissions retained; C: 11/11 specified attacks contained and 16/16 audit trails complete locally. | Each claim must preserve its experiment-specific population and method. |
| Operational demonstration | Live collector/logging/rule fallback was observed in 200 records. | All records were bootstrap-rule mode and all LSTM scores were null. |
| Future hypotheses | External transfer, live LSTM behavior, LLM/RCA effectiveness, authenticated governance, and safe recovery in real operations. | State as testable future work, not present outcome. |

## Non-negotiable limitations to retain in the supervisor-review version

1. **Synthetic benchmark dependence:** A is 200 nominal and 120 fault windows across six constructed scenarios; clustered windows limit inferential/generalization meaning. Full rules structurally overlap the simulation predicate.
2. **Baseline integrity:** only current raw-reconstructable Full Rules, Isolation Forest, LSTM, and Combined results belong in the main table. Narrow/Resource figures are historical unless independently reconstructed.
3. **Pilot diagnosis scale:** B contains six cases across seven configurations and two strict-ITT `UNASSESSABLE` provider omissions. It does not establish causal or general operational benefit.
4. **Local approval boundary:** C tests a finite local harness. The approver is an unauthenticated label, not a verified identity or dual authorization; action/probe behavior is a fixed local demonstration.
5. **No live LSTM evidence:** live telemetry is 200 bootstrap-rule records with null LSTM scores; no accuracy, false-positive, alert/hour, or continuous-operation inference is possible.
6. **No Experiment D result:** AnoMod archive provenance does not constitute data transfer, benchmark execution, or external performance. Contract-valid D remains blocked.
7. **Integrity/provenance incomplete:** the historical manifest is stale for seven entries and the working tree is dirty. Hash equality, where observed, is not a scientific rerun.
8. **Figure validation limitation:** all Phase 4 PDFs/PNGs were visually inspected, but SVGs received XML parsing rather than independent graphical rasterization. Retain this limitation in the figure-validation record; rendering success alone is not publication readiness.

## Statements to exclude from present-tense conclusions

- Novel LSTM architecture or causal representation.
- Production-ready, secure, NIST-compliant, or authenticated dual-approval recovery.
- Autonomous safe remediation, replay-proof authorization, or universal attack prevention.
- Live LSTM detection, real-world false-alarm rates, alarms/hour, or external generalization.
- LLM/multi-agent diagnostic superiority, reduced hallucination, or causal-precision improvement.
- AnoMod or other external performance, even as “preliminary,” absent valid D artifacts.

## Future-work framing

Future work can test: a prospectively defined external protocol compatible with the 24-step contract; independently labeled streaming live-LSTM evaluation; adequately powered diagnosis studies with retained provider evidence; authenticated multi-party approval and realistic remote remediation controls; and independent security testing. These are hypotheses and protocol targets, not contributions already demonstrated.
