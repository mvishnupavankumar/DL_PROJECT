### Claim Classification Statuses:
- **Supported**: Supported directly by machine-readable data, logs, and executable code without caveats.
- **Supported with Qualification**: Empirically verified, but requires explicit statement of boundary conditions, experimental scope, or statistical power limitations.

- **Blocked**: Excluded from immediate paper claims due to methodological, contract, or dataset blockers.

2. **Historical Validation-Window Count Reconciliation**:
   - *Previous Reports*: Cited "~2 windows", "$\le 5$ windows", and "8 windows".
   - *Verified Truth*: Traced in [`EXPERIMENT_D_ZERO_SHOT_CONTRACT_AUDIT.md`](file:///c:/DL_PROJECT/research/audits/EXPERIMENT_D_ZERO_SHOT_CONTRACT_AUDIT.md). For $W=24$: exactly 0 eligible windows exist.
   - *Resolution*: Experiment D is excluded from paper claims under Path 3, eliminating small-sample validation variance from manuscript results.
3. **Detection Confusion Matrix Reconciliation (QC-1)**:
